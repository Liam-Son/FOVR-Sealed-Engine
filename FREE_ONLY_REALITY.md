# FOVR v18 — Free-Only Reality

The old 104-symbol historical options source is no longer treated as live. Contemporary mirrors document that its original GitHub/CDN disappeared in 2026. Only a preserved SPY/IWM/QQQ long-history copy is reliably documented.

### Historical survivorship
- QuantRocket free learning bundle: 2007–2011, all US stocks, includes later-delisted names.
- Free S&P500 PIT pipeline: 2015–present historical constituents + SEC PIT fundamentals.

### Individual-stock options
- ThetaData FREE: 1 year EOD US stock/options. Use for the 36-stock FOVR universe and append daily.
- SPY/IWM/QQQ preserved 2008–2025 options: methodology/stress validation only, not a 36-stock historical FOVR backtest.

A free source cannot create a commercial options entitlement, historical prime-broker borrow inventory that was never archived, or a live track record for dates that have already passed.
